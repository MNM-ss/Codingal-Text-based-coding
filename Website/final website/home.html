What is M.O.M ?

M.O.M. stands for "Missions Operation Management". The agency was founded and run by the Nelson siblings' Mom until her disappearance. Now Shiloh and her siblings run the agency with a growing collection of agents.

The agency responds to and monitors distress calls from numerous worlds called Raelos - combatting evil and fighting for truth and justice. They willingly appear in these different Raelos to combat the problems it’s people or the environment is facing.
