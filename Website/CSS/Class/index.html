~📍Jujutsu Kaisen ~

Gege Akutami

Jujutsu Kaisen is a dark fantasy series created by Gege Akutami. The story follows Yuji Itadori, a high school student with unusual strength. After he swallows a cursed object, a finger of Ryomen Sukuna, he becomes the host of a dangerous curse. Instead of being executed, he joins Tokyo Jujutsu High to fight curses. The series mixes action, horror, and emotion. You see how fear and regret give birth to curses, and how sorcerers risk their lives to protect others.

Yuji trains under Satoru Gojo, the strongest jujutsu sorcerer. He fights alongside Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki. Each character has a clear motive. Yuji wants to give people a proper death. Megumi seeks justice in his own strict way. Nobara values pride and self respect. Their teamwork drives many battles. The fights focus on strategy, cursed energy control, and domain expansions. Every battle has rules. You must understand those rules to survive.

The power system stands out. Cursed energy comes from negative human emotions. Sorcerers learn to control and shape this energy into techniques. Some inherit family techniques. Others develop unique skills. Domain Expansion represents the peak of ability. Inside a domain, attacks almost always hit. This system creates tension because you know one mistake leads to death. The Shibuya Incident arc shows this clearly. Major characters fall. The world shifts into chaos.

The series also explores moral conflict. Yuji struggles with guilt over the lives Sukuna takes. Gojo believes in reforming the jujutsu world. Antagonists like Suguru Geto argue that non sorcerers cause suffering. These clashes raise hard questions about value and sacrifice. You see both sides argue with logic and pain. That depth gives the story weight beyond fights.

Noteworthy point: The animation by MAPPA raises the impact of battles. Fluid movement and sharp choreography pull you into each scene. Music and sound design intensify emotional moments without excess.

Another key note. The series gained global popularity fast. The anime adaptation broke streaming records in multiple regions. Strong characters and high stakes keep viewers invested.
